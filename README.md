# List of Free Language Practice Chatbots
A list of free voice enabled chatbots that help language learners practice conversation.
- English
- German
- Mandarin Chinese
- Spanish
- Thai

<br>

Many language learners learn vocabulary and grammar in a language school, but may find it hard to get conversation practice. Also, many may be shy or anxious about making mistakes in front of others.

The web apps on this list try to solve these problems by providing a free, private and low-stress way to practice conversation on a phone or on a desktop computer. These apps support voice in, voice out. 

The chat system is multimodal. This means that it supports image uploads. Imagine sending a photo of a menu to the chatbot and then roleplaying ordering from that menu.

Created to support motivated, self-directed learners. There's no lesson plan, no gamification, no streaks, and no external prompts to come back.
